# Privacy Policy for Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon

**Last updated: September 12, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon** ("Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon," "the game," "we," "our," or "us") accesses, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when players use the game and its optional online services.

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon is developed and maintained by **IntoTheX / ErebusPlays**.

This policy applies to Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon, including its optional **Wayfarer Network** features.

---

## 1. Summary

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon is primarily a game that can be played without enabling the Wayfarer Network.

The optional Wayfarer Network provides online features including:

- Wayfarer account registration;
- player usernames;
- nearby player visibility on an in-game map;
- private player-to-player chat;
- player trading or other supported online interactions;
- Safety Reports and moderation.

Wayfarer features are optional.

Players who do not enable Wayfarer visibility are not required to provide location information for normal offline gameplay.

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon does not sell personal information and does not use Wayfarer location or chat data for advertising.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on which features a player chooses to use, Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon may process the following information.

### Wayfarer Account Information

Players who choose to register for the Wayfarer Network may provide or be assigned:

- an email address;
- a Wayfarer username;
- an Account ID;
- one or more Character IDs associated with game saves or characters.

The Account ID identifies the Wayfarer account associated with the player's registered email address.

A Character ID identifies an individual game character or game save.

A player's username may be associated with their Character ID and Wayfarer account.

This information is used to provide Wayfarer services, authenticate players, maintain account functionality, support player interactions, enforce Wayfarer rules, and prevent abuse or sanction evasion.

---

## 3. Game and Save Data

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon stores gameplay information necessary to maintain player progress.

This may include information such as:

- game progress;
- characters;
- inventory;
- equipment;
- achievements or badges;
- unlocked content;
- game settings and preferences;
- other gameplay-related information.

Most gameplay and save information is stored on the player's device.

Depending on the player's Android device and operating-system settings, some application data may also be included in operating-system backup or restoration services.

Such platform-level backup services are controlled by the applicable device or platform provider and may be governed by that provider's own privacy policies.

---

## 4. Wayfarer Network

The **Wayfarer Network** is an optional online feature that allows participating Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon players to discover and interact with one another.

Players do not need to enable Wayfarer visibility to play the game's normal offline features.

---

## 5. Approximate Location

When a player enables **Wayfarer Visibility**, Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon may request access to the device's **approximate location**.

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon does not require precise location permission for the Wayfarer Network.

Approximate location is used to determine where participating players should appear relative to one another on the Wayfarer in-game map.

Location access is used only for Wayfarer functionality and is not used for advertising or marketing.

---

## 6. Location Obfuscation

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon does not intentionally display a player's reported device location directly to other Wayfarer players.

Before a player's position is displayed to other Wayfarer participants, the displayed map position is intentionally displaced from the player's reported location by approximately **400–500 meters**.

This location-obfuscation system is intended to reduce the risk that another player could determine a participant's actual physical position using the Wayfarer map.

However, no location-obfuscation system can guarantee that a player's real-world location can never be inferred.

Players should therefore avoid sharing their exact physical location, home address, workplace, school, or other sensitive location information through Wayfarer Chat.

---

## 7. Location Retention

Wayfarer does not maintain a historical database of player locations.

When Wayfarer Visibility is active, the Wayfarer service temporarily maintains the player's current approximate location as part of the active session.

Location information is processed only for the real-time operation of the Wayfarer Network.

When a player:

- disables Wayfarer Visibility;
- disconnects from Wayfarer; or
- closes the game,

the active Wayfarer position is removed from the service after a short transition period, normally within several seconds.

Historical player movement or location history is not intentionally retained by the Wayfarer service.

---

## 8. Information Visible to Other Wayfarer Players

When Wayfarer Visibility is enabled, other participating players may be able to see information necessary to provide the Wayfarer experience.

This may include:

- the player's Wayfarer username;
- an intentionally displaced representation of the player's approximate location;
- supported public character information;
- information required for supported Wayfarer interaction features.

The player's raw device location is not intentionally displayed to other players.

---

## 9. Wayfarer Chat

Wayfarer allows participating players to communicate using private in-game messaging.

Normal Wayfarer chat messages are stored on the participating players' devices.

Messages are encrypted and relayed through the Wayfarer service to allow delivery between participating players.

The Wayfarer service does not normally maintain a server-side conversation history of private chats.

Normal private message content is not intended to be accessible to the developer, server operators, or moderators unless a participant deliberately submits the conversation through the **Safety Report** system described below.

Players should never use Wayfarer Chat to share information they do not want another participant to know.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- passwords;
- financial information;
- home addresses;
- exact physical locations;
- government identification information;
- other highly sensitive personal information.

---

## 10. Safety Reports

Wayfarer includes a Safety Report system intended to help protect players and enforce community rules.

A participant in a conversation may voluntarily submit a Safety Report when they believe another player has violated Wayfarer safety or community rules.

When a Safety Report is submitted, the most recent **30 messages** associated with the reported conversation may be included with the report and transmitted to authorized moderators for review.

A Safety Report may also contain information necessary to identify the involved players, including:

- the reporting player's username;
- the reported player's username;
- Account IDs where necessary;
- Character IDs where necessary;
- moderation information;
- the moderation decision;
- warnings, restrictions, or sanctions associated with the report.

This is an intentional exception to Wayfarer's normal private-chat handling.

Conversation content submitted through a Safety Report becomes accessible to authorized moderators for the purpose of investigating the report.

---

## 11. Moderation and Enforcement

Authorized moderators may review submitted Safety Reports and determine whether a report is valid or invalid.

Confirmed violations may result in enforcement actions.

Depending on the severity or frequency of violations, enforcement actions may include:

- warnings;
- temporary chat restrictions;
- temporary trading restrictions;
- other temporary Wayfarer restrictions;
- stronger sanctions for repeated or serious violations;
- deletion of an affected character save in cases involving repeated or serious violations, where permitted by the applicable game and community rules.

Moderation information is used solely for purposes such as operating and protecting the Wayfarer Network, enforcing community rules, investigating abuse, protecting users, and preventing sanction evasion.

Moderation information is not used for advertising or marketing.

---

## 12. Moderation Record Retention

Safety Reports and violation history may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- investigate reported behavior;
- enforce Wayfarer safety and community rules;
- identify repeat violations;
- prevent sanction evasion;
- prevent a user from avoiding sanctions merely by creating a new character or game save;
- protect players and the integrity of the Wayfarer Network.

Because Character IDs may change when a player creates a new game save, relevant moderation history may remain associated with the underlying Wayfarer account rather than only with a single Character ID.

Limited moderation or enforcement records may also be retained after an account deletion where reasonably necessary for security, abuse prevention, fraud prevention, enforcement of previous sanctions, or prevention of repeated violations.

Any information retained for these purposes will not be used for unrelated advertising or marketing purposes.

## 13. Internet and Network Access

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon may connect to the internet to provide supported functionality.

This may include:

- Wayfarer Network services;
- account authentication;
- player interactions;
- chat delivery;
- Safety Reports;
- game news or announcements;
- update information;
- other online game functionality.

Normal internet communications may result in service providers receiving technical networking information such as an IP address, request information, connection information, and timestamps.

Such information may be processed as necessary to establish and maintain network communications, protect service security, diagnose technical problems, or prevent abuse.

---

## 14. Advertising

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon does not currently use Wayfarer location information, private chat messages, email addresses, or Wayfarer account identifiers for advertising or behavioral advertising.

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon does not sell Wayfarer user information to advertisers.

If advertising or advertising-related data practices are introduced in a future version of Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon, this Privacy Policy will be updated as required before those practices are introduced.

---

## 15. Analytics and Tracking

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon does not use Wayfarer location or private message content to create advertising profiles or track users across unrelated applications or websites.

Technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, or maintain online services may be processed for those purposes.

---

## 16. Sharing of Information

Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be disclosed or transmitted when necessary to provide functionality intentionally requested by the player.

For example:

- an obfuscated Wayfarer map position may be displayed to other participating Wayfarer players when Visibility is enabled;
- private messages are delivered to their intended recipients;
- information submitted through a Safety Report is made available to authorized moderators;
- information may be processed by infrastructure providers necessary to operate online services.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect users, investigate abuse, enforce applicable rules, or protect the security and integrity of Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon and the Wayfarer Network.

---

## 17. Security

We take reasonable measures designed to reduce unnecessary collection of personal information and protect information processed through Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon.

These measures include practices such as:

- limiting Wayfarer location access to approximate location;
- intentionally displacing player positions displayed on the Wayfarer map;
- avoiding historical storage of Wayfarer location information;
- encrypting supported network communications;
- limiting normal private-chat storage to participating users' devices;
- restricting access to submitted Safety Reports to authorized moderation purposes.

However, no electronic transmission, software system, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Players should take reasonable precautions when interacting with other users online.

Players who create a Wayfarer account may request deletion of their Wayfarer account and associated personal data.

An account deletion option will be available from within Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon through the Wayfarer account controls.

Players may also initiate an account deletion request using the following external resource:

**Account deletion page:**

Account deletion is intended to remove the Wayfarer account and personal information associated with that account that is no longer required for legitimate purposes.

Certain limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

- security;
- fraud prevention;
- abuse prevention;
- enforcement of previously issued sanctions;
- preventing sanction or moderation evasion;
- compliance with applicable legal obligations.

Where information is retained for these limited purposes, it will not be used for unrelated purposes such as advertising or marketing.

Deleting a Wayfarer account does not necessarily remove local game saves stored on the player's device.

Local game data may be removed separately through available in-game controls, Android application-storage controls, or by uninstalling the application, subject to any device backup or restoration settings controlled by the player.

## 19. Player Choices

Players control whether they participate in the Wayfarer Network.

Players may choose not to enable Wayfarer Visibility.

Players may also revoke Reclaimed Pixel Dungeon's location permission through Android's application permission settings.

Revoking location access or disabling Wayfarer Visibility may prevent location-dependent Wayfarer features from functioning but does not prevent players from using game functionality that does not require those features.
